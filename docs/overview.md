# GeoPoliticalMedia — Overview And Launch Plan

Updated: September 11, 2026. This is a proposed operating plan, not a deployed app or an earnings forecast. Official platform documentation was checked on this date; account access, prices, and policies should be rechecked before launch.

## 1. What We Are Building

**GeoPoliticalMedia is the parent organization and distribution hub for your apps and services, and the name of its news channel. Five planned channels produce YouTube videos and Shorts: GeoPoliticalMedia, Geo Studios, Geo Labs, Geo Data, and Geo Gaming.**

The GeoPoliticalMedia app should serve two audiences:

- **Public visitors:** discover the organization, browse apps/services, open or download a product, watch its channels, and contact the business
- **You as the operator:** manage products, plan videos, track production jobs and spending, review performance, and see profit by business unit

The parent organization can support future software products and services while the GeoPoliticalMedia channel focuses on news. Geo Studios, Geo Labs, Geo Data, and Geo Gaming can each use their own visual identity with “A GeoPoliticalMedia Service” as an endorsement.

| Entity | Purpose | First Revenue Paths |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GeoPoliticalMedia Organization | Own the portfolio, brand, distribution site, and shared operations | Revenue from its individual business units |
| GeoPoliticalMedia Channel | News, weather, economics, politics, global news, and financial news | YouTube monetization after approval; later suitable sponsors and memberships |
| Geo Studios | Studio work, video production, photography, design, web design, app design, and art | YouTube monetization after approval; later suitable sponsors, creative services, and resources |
| Geo Labs | AI, coding, technology, engineering, science, and science facts | YouTube monetization after approval; later suitable sponsors and educational resources |
| Geo Data | Data, analytics, APIs, and practical data workflows | YouTube monetization after approval; later suitable sponsors, tools, and resources |
| Geo Gaming | Gaming, gameplay, guides, and game analysis | YouTube monetization after approval; later suitable sponsors and memberships |
| Future Apps And Services | Solve specific customer problems | Subscriptions, one-time purchases, or service fees when a product is validated |

Keep revenue and expenses separate by business unit, including the GeoPoliticalMedia channel. An internal transfer from a channel to its parent is not new consolidated revenue.

**Current repository:** branding assets and concept galleries exist, including [the proof-of-concept logo studies](../public/assets/concepts/poc/README.md) and [alternate logo directions](../public/assets/concepts/alternate/README.md). There is no implemented application, authenticated dashboard, connected channel, or production pipeline yet.

## 2. Starting Strategy

Give each service its own channel brief, audience promise, and manageable production schedule. Share production tools and operations through GeoPoliticalMedia. Build enough software to organize the work and measure its economics; let actual production experience determine which automation comes next.

Use news briefings for GeoPoliticalMedia, creative tutorials for Geo Studios, technology and science demonstrations for Geo Labs, data tutorials and analysis for Geo Data, and gameplay, guides, and game analysis for Geo Gaming. Keep each channel's audience promise consistent.

Proposed combined first-month output: **eight Shorts and two longer videos total across the selected channels**, adjusted downward if quality or the spending cap requires it. Start with one or two channels; adding channel ideas does not multiply the production target. Compare treatments within each audience and track each channel separately. These are experiments, not a promised growth formula.

Each video needs an original hook, a coherent script, deliberate editing, and a reason to watch beyond “this was generated by AI.” Create a new edit and narrative purpose for each Short instead of uploading near-identical crops repeatedly.

### Proposed Technical Approach

Use one TypeScript web application initially: **Next.js, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, private object storage, and a small server-side worker for external jobs**. Keep external providers behind adapters so the organization can change tools without rewriting the entire app.

Firebase App Hosting is a deployment candidate with built-in Next.js support and Firebase integration; it requires billing-enabled setup. Select the actual hosting configuration and review its expected cost when implementation begins. The current XAMPP directory does not make a local workstation a production hosting plan. [Firebase App Hosting](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-hosting)

Use Owner, Editor, and Analyst roles. Public visitors must never access costs, channel tokens, unreleased media, or financial reports. Keep secrets server-side and media/CSV archives in private storage or outside Apache’s document root—not in `public/assets`, and not merely in a Git-ignored folder under `htdocs`.

Suggested collections: `business_units`, `products`, `channels`, `series`, `videos`, `assets`, `generation_jobs`, `expenses`, `revenue_records`, `analytics_imports`, and `daily_metrics`.

Create separate business-unit records for the GeoPoliticalMedia channel, Geo Studios, Geo Labs, Geo Data, and Geo Gaming. Associate each channel, series, video, and direct expense with its owning unit. The shared production dashboard should filter by service; keep aggregate portfolio totals separate from each channel's metrics.

Follow your document convention: app-owned `id` in the `Type_Number_Name_Date_UUID` shape, with an integer `number` matching the number inside the ID. Allocate numbers and create documents in a transaction. Keep `youtube_video_id`, `youtube_channel_id`, and `higgsfield_request_id` as separate fields. Firestore transactions provide atomic updates and retry conflicting reads. [Firestore transactions](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/manage-data/transactions)

Store money in integer minor units with currency, dates, and source records. Never use a display title as a financial join key.

## 4. How Codex And Higgsfield Fit Together

| Responsibility | Tool Or Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Build the app, import reports, calculate metrics, maintain scripts | Codex |
| Draft ideas, original scripts, shot lists, prompt variations, captions, metadata | Codex, with your editorial direction |
| Generate footage and supporting images | Higgsfield web tools or official CLI |

### First Higgsfield Setup

The official CLI provides an agent-friendly route; Higgsfield’s integration documentation recommends it for Codex. When ready to configure the workstation, use the documented setup:

```powershell
npm install -g @higgsfield/cli
higgsfield auth login
```

Complete sign-in in the browser. Optional companion skills are available through `npx skills add higgsfield-ai/skills`; inspect the skills being installed. These commands are setup instructions, not actions performed by this document. [Higgsfield CLI](https://higgsfield.ai/cli), [Higgsfield agent integrations](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp)

The official CLI supports discovering available models/parameters, estimating generation cost, checking jobs, and inspecting credits. It also documents narration, reframing, and assembly workflows. Have Codex discover the current model schema rather than hard-code a model name and assume every model supports identical durations, sound, or aspect ratios. [Official CLI documentation](https://github.com/higgsfield-ai/cli)

For each episode:

1. Write the hook, script, shot list, and visual continuity brief before generating footage
2. Estimate the full batch, including expected retries; set a per-episode cap and a maximum number of rerolls
3. Generate a small reference/style test, then the approved shots
4. Save each job ID, settings, estimate, result, charged credits, and downloaded file
5. Assemble the video, narration, music, captions, and thumbnail; review the complete export
6. Upload privately or as a draft, inspect the result, then schedule the approved version

Use 9:16 for the Shorts edit and 16:9 for the longer video where appropriate. Preserve important subjects during reframing, and check captions and audio on a phone. Log failed and discarded generations, including gross charges and confirmed refunds. Only net unreimbursed charges count as cost; successfully generated clips you discard still consume the resources spent on them.

### Later API Integration

Higgsfield has a public asynchronous API. A future server worker can authenticate with server-side credentials, submit a request, persist `request_id`, and poll or handle a verified webhook. This is optional after the assisted CLI workflow works. [Higgsfield API documentation](https://docs.higgsfield.ai/docs)

A timeout is not proof a generation failed. Retrieve the existing job before resubmitting, bound retries, and download completed media into your own archive. The API documents cost estimation in credits/USD, refunds for failed/moderated requests, and retention of outputs for at least seven days. Distinguish a credit refund from cash returned to a bank account, and keep pending adjustments separate from confirmed ones. Temporary provider URLs are not the asset archive. [API billing and retention](https://docs.higgsfield.ai/docs/concepts/billing-and-retention)

### Credits And Commercial Use

Website Unlimited/free allowances do not carry over to CLI/MCP automation. Generation costs vary by model and settings, and subscription credits expire rather than rolling forward. Track actual subscription invoices, top-ups, expirations, and generation charges. Keep consumer/CLI spending and Cloud API charges separate until the account confirms how its balances relate. Current subscription dollar prices were not reliably exposed in the public pricing response, so this plan does not invent a tier price. [Credit rules](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credits-work), [Current pricing](https://higgsfield.ai/pricing)

Higgsfield permits commercial use of outputs, including YouTube monetization. That does not clear third-party footage, music, voices, or likenesses, guarantee exclusive copyright, or guarantee YouTube approval. Keep source/licensing records and use voices and music you are entitled to publish. [Commercial-use guidance](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially), [Terms](https://higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement)

## 5. YouTube Launch And Monetization

Create separate GeoPoliticalMedia, Geo Studios, Geo Labs, Geo Data, and Geo Gaming channels under accounts you control as their pilots become ready. For each, set up the handle, banner, description, contact details, channel permissions, two-step verification, and required account features. Use permissions for collaborators rather than sharing the owner password. Configure audience settings accurately.

Current milestone structure:

| Milestone | Main Thresholds | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expanded YPP, where available | 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, plus 3,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months **or** 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days | Access to eligible fan-funding/Shopping features |
| Ad/Premium Revenue Sharing | 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months **or** 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days | Eligibility to apply for the relevant revenue-sharing features |

Thresholds are not automatic acceptance: country availability, account requirements, policy review, AdSense setup, and feature/module eligibility also matter. Shorts Feed watch time does not count toward the long-form watch-hour route. [Expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en), [YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)

Shorts earnings are not retroactive for views before accepting the Shorts Monetization Module. Use the creator revenue reported in Studio rather than trying to infer your payout from an ad adjacent to one Short. [Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)

YouTube evaluates original value and meaningful variation. Mass-produced templates, repetitive low-value videos, and minimally changed reused material can fail monetization review. An AI tool subscription or permission to reuse footage does not by itself make a channel eligible. Build original scripts, distinctive stories, intentional narration, and substantive edits. [Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)

Disclose meaningful synthetic/altered realistic content using the current AI-use disclosure controls during upload. Clearly fantastical content and basic script assistance are different cases. Disclosure itself does not disqualify monetization. If publishing factual explainers, verify claims and distinguish reconstructions from real footage. [AI-content disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

The release review should answer: Is the video original and coherent? Are claims and assets checked? Are audio/captions clear? Is the title/thumbnail accurate? Are audience and AI disclosures appropriate?

## 6. Viewing Revenue And Profit Breakdowns

### Start In YouTube Studio

1. Open **YouTube Studio → Analytics → Revenue**
2. Choose a date range and compare Videos and Shorts separately
3. Open **Advanced Mode / See More**, choose content/series and relevant metrics, and export the current view
4. Import the report into GeoPoliticalMedia and join each video to its production costs
5. Reconcile the month against finalized AdSense earnings and actual bank receipts

Studio reports estimated creator revenue, not business profit. Revenue usually has about a two-day delay and can change. Prior-month finalized earnings appear in AdSense for YouTube between the 7th and 12th. Keep **estimated earnings**, **finalized earnings**, and **cash received** as separate amounts; never add them together as three income streams. [Revenue reports](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314488?hl=en-GB)

Advanced Mode exports have row limits; the documented download limit is 500 rows. Check for truncation and use smaller date/content slices if needed. Save the raw export, filters, period, currency, channel, and import timestamp. [Advanced Mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9717005?hl=en)

### Dashboard Views

| View | Useful Breakdowns |
| --- | --- |
| Channel Overview | Revenue, costs, operating profit, cash received, budget remaining, monetization status |
| Format Comparison | Shorts versus longer videos: views, engaged views where applicable, watch time, retention, revenue, cost, contribution |
| Video Detail | Title, publish date, series, provider jobs, spend, views, retention, revenue, profit to date |
| Series Comparison | Audience response and financial results at matched 7-day and 28-day ages |
| Monthly Close | Estimated versus finalized earnings, invoice totals, adjustments, actual receipts |
| Portfolio | GeoPoliticalMedia channel, Geo Studios, Geo Labs, Geo Data, Geo Gaming, and future apps/services, with shared overhead allocated once |

Filter by date, channel, format, series, and monetization state. Show reporting currency and “data available through” dates. Separate zero from missing or not-yet-reported data. An unmonetized channel should say **Not Monetized**, rather than implying an API error means zero audience activity.

For creators discussing their earnings on YouTube, compare reporting period, format, country mix, monetization status, tool costs, sponsorships, and whether they show gross revenue or net profit. Public view counts cannot reveal another channel’s actual private revenue or profit.

### Later Read-Only YouTube Connection

Start with CSV imports. Add owner-authorized OAuth when repeated imports are a burden:

- `yt-analytics.readonly` for audience/performance reports
- `yt-analytics-monetary.readonly` for monetary reports when the channel is in YPP
- Query the authenticated owner’s channel and use supported metric/dimension combinations

The canonical channel-report documentation supports monetary metrics, but a non-YPP channel can receive a 403 for a monetary query. Treat authorization/eligibility failures as actionable connection states. [Analytics channel reports](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports)

Preserve raw imports separately from canonical metrics. For daily data, use channel, date, video, format, metric, and currency as the canonical identity; store source/provenance as metadata, not as another additive revenue stream. Define which verified source wins when CSV and API cover the same data. Replace overlapping estimates rather than summing them. Keep monthly aggregates and channel totals at their own reporting level: reconcile against daily/video data instead of adding them to it, and never fabricate daily allocations from a monthly export.

Re-query recent dates for adjustments. Do not assume the latest calendar day is available; mixed-metric queries stop at the latest date for which all requested metrics exist. [Reports query reference](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference/reports/query)

Keep upload permissions separate from reporting. A later custom uploader may need an API compliance audit: uploads from unverified API projects created after July 28, 2020 are restricted to private viewing. Use Studio scheduling first. [YouTube upload API](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert)

## 7. Profit Model And Spending Controls

Use creator revenue, not advertiser CPM:

```text
Estimated YouTube Revenue = Reported Creator Revenue
Operating Profit = YouTube Revenue + External Channel Income
                   - Direct Production Costs - Allocated Shared Costs
Economic Profit = Operating Profit - Value Of Owner Time
Cash Movement = Actual Cash Receipts - Actual Cash Payments
```

RPM is creator revenue per 1,000 views; for Shorts it uses engaged views. CPM is an advertiser metric. Do not apply long-video RPM to Shorts, and do not subtract YouTube's share again from already-reported creator revenue. Total estimated revenue and its component categories must not be counted twice. [Revenue metric definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314357?hl=en)

Record Higgsfield, Codex, hosting/storage, narration/music, editing, contractor fees, and net unreimbursed generation costs. Assign each invoice to the business unit or allocate it transparently. If included subscription credits are allocated to videos, that is a distribution of the subscription expense—not an extra charge on top of the same invoice. Reconcile refunds and keep unused/expired credit cost visible.

### Illustrative Monthly Example

The following are **invented arithmetic inputs, not tool quotes, expected RPMs, view forecasts, or income promises**. Replace them with your invoices and channel data.

Assume eight Shorts and two longer videos total across the selected channels; $120 fixed/shared monthly costs; $6 extra direct cost per Short; $30 per longer video. Those extra costs exclude anything already covered in the fixed amount.

`Monthly Cost = $120 + (8 × $6) + (2 × $30) = $228`

For illustration only, use $0.05 Shorts RPM and $3.00 longer-video RPM:

| Scenario | Shorts Engaged Views | Longer-Video Views | YouTube Revenue | Operating Profit Before Owner Time/Tax |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Before Any Monetization, No Other Income | Any | Any | $0 | -$228 |
| Example A, After Monetization | 300,000 | 30,000 | $105 | -$123 |
| Example B, After Monetization | 2,000,000 | 200,000 | $700 | $472 |

At those hypothetical RPMs, covering $228 through only longer videos needs 76,000 views; through only Shorts it needs 4,560,000 engaged views. Real costs and realized RPMs will vary.

If owner time is 20 hours valued at $20/hour, subtract another $400 when evaluating economic profit. A profitable cash month can still be a poor use of your time. Record app, affiliate, and sponsor revenue separately rather than assuming it will appear.

Proposed combined pilot ceiling: **$250 for the first month across the selected channels, subject to actual account prices**. Adding channels does not increase this proposed allowance. If fixed commitments already exceed it, reduce scope or choose a different cap before starting. Disable surprise auto-refill, estimate before generation, and stop new paid jobs when the remaining approved budget is exhausted. A spending limit is a control, not a revenue target.

## 9. Keeping It Running With Codex

Make reusable production instructions after a few real episodes reveal a stable process. Candidate skills: `geo-script`, `geo-shot-plan`, `geo-release-review`, and `geo-finance-review`. A skill can package instructions and supporting scripts; it does not itself provide a persistent scheduler. [Codex skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills)

| Cadence | Operating Work |
| --- | --- |
| Each Production Session | Check remaining budget; process a bounded batch; save outputs; review failed jobs before retrying |
| Twice Weekly | Script/edit/publish the next approved batch; maintain a small release buffer |
| Weekly | Review retention, cost per finished minute, profit by format, job failures, and backlog |
| Monthly | Reconcile Studio/AdSense/invoices; allocate shared costs; decide which subscriptions and series to continue |
| Each App Release | Review the change, check affected functionality and permissions, deploy, confirm it works, retain rollback instructions |

Suggested Codex prompts:

- “Read the GeoPoliticalMedia idea bank. Develop one news explainer with dated sources, a clear timeline, and context for viewers”
- “Read the Geo Studios idea bank. Develop one practical creative tutorial with a clear before-and-after result”
- “Read the Geo Labs idea bank. Develop one AI, coding, technology, engineering, or science demonstration with checked claims and a reproducible result”
- “Read the Geo Data idea bank. Develop one data or API tutorial with a documented dataset, working example, and clear chart”
- “Read the Geo Gaming idea bank. Develop one gameplay guide with original footage and a practical takeaway”
- “Turn this approved script into a shot list and Higgsfield prompts. Estimate every generation and stay within the episode’s remaining budget”
- “Read the latest private analytics export and expense records. Reconcile totals and report revenue, costs, profit, missing data, and the next three experiments”
- “Review the failed generation jobs. Retrieve existing provider statuses before retrying, and identify any risk of duplicate charges”

After the workflow is stable, configure scheduled reviews explicitly. Local scheduled tasks need the computer on and the app running to access local files. Use an always-on hosted worker/scheduler for production jobs that must continue independently of the desktop; Codex can help build and maintain it. Scheduling ideas in this document have **not** been activated. [Scheduled tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app)

Use isolated worktrees for separate app changes to protect unfinished work. Keep the selected Codex model and speed settings, and measure whether a task’s cost is justified. [Codex worktrees](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/environments/git-worktrees)

Operations should preserve job IDs, retry limits, cost logs, and durable status. Track last successful sync, expired connections, failed renders, low storage, and budget exhaustion. Notify on meaningful failure or required action rather than emitting an unchanged report repeatedly.

Back up scripts, manifests, invoices, reports, and final media. Keep code and templates in Git; keep large footage, secrets, and private financial exports in appropriate private storage. Check that backups can actually be restored. Renewals, access reviews, and monthly financial reconciliation need an owner even when production is automated.
