# GeoPoliticalMedia

GeoPoliticalMedia is the media company within **GeoCorp**, the parent organization. It focuses on world affairs, politics, economics, weather, and independent storytelling.

## Current website

The standalone landing page is authored in `dist/index.html` and `dist/styles.css`. Its G/Earth/red-orbit identity comes from the approved logo reference in `public/assets/concepts/finalize/`. It links to the parent GeoCorp site.

The parent rotating-globe website and Expo mobile app live entirely in the sibling `GeoCorp/` repository. GeoPoliticalMedia has no Expo dependency and does not serve the parent app.

## Editorial direction

Explain the forces behind the news. Check facts against dated primary sources, distinguish reporting from opinion, and label simulated or illustrative media. Topics and recurring formats are in [the idea bank](ideas.md). No new reports, channel connections, or publishing schedules are implied by the landing page.

## Retained media archive

[Logo concepts](../public/assets/concepts/README.md) and [video samples](../public/assets/videos/samples/README.md) preserve the existing development material. The production archive also includes sample work for other GeoCorp brands; GeoPoliticalMedia retains these as media production assets. Only the landing page and its approved logo are included in the website's `dist/` output.

The broader portfolio operations proposal now belongs to [GeoCorp](../../GeoCorp/docs/overview.md). Authentication, finance, automated generation, and channel dashboards remain planned work.
