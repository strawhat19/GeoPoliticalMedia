# Core Earth

Built-in image editing with two references: Arctic Coordinate for the GC geometry and the approved GPM Polar logo for its Earth. No paid APIs or purchases.

## Exact prompt

Use case: logo-brand / compositing. Produce ONE standalone GC logo icon combining the two supplied references.

IMAGE 1 is the structural/design reference: ARCTIC COORDINATE. Keep its recognizable white OUTER C sweep and nested INNER G with a broad attached horizontal crossbar, plus the small ELECTRIC CYAN right-angle coordinate corner at the upper right. Preserve this specific refined nested GC family, the crisp white geometry and the confident clean negative space. Do not replace it with just a generic single G.

IMAGE 2 is the EARTH DONOR ONLY. Use the approved natural Earth from that image, preserving its western-hemisphere view with BOTH North America and South America prominently recognizable and Greenland above, natural blue oceans and green/tan land, delicate white clouds, believable spherical shading and the same overall Earth appearance. Treat the donor globe as the source insert, not a cue to invent a different planet. Keep the actual land shapes, globe orientation and cloud character as close as possible, scaling the sphere only to fit the new GC mark. Ignore and remove the donor's original single G and RED ORBIT; neither is wanted in this variation. Do not add any other orbital ring.

One strong integrated mark, not a row of separate GC and Earth symbols. White arcs and cyan coordinate corner remain unmistakable against an opaque UNIFORM deep navy #050F1C background. The Earth sits within or behind the GC, with clean negative space separating relevant white strokes. Keep both continents readable and avoid a large white bar crossing their centers. No glow beyond a very fine contained polished edge; no halo haze, sparkles, background stars, nebula, mockup, border, title or numbering. Center one icon at about 72 percent of a square canvas with generous safe margins. Only the GC symbol and the planet; absolutely NO words, NO company name, NO domain, NO wordmark, NO captions or typography. Output one high-resolution square PNG.

VARIATION:
Closest-to-original insertion. Keep Arctic Coordinate outer/inner arc shapes and stroke weights nearly exactly. Place the donor Earth as a modest round insert in the central negative-space counter of the inner G. Globe diameter about 42 percent of the outer logo diameter. Position it slightly upper-left of the horizontal G terminal so the planet is fully visible rather than bisected; allow a clean narrow dark breathing gap around the globe. The planet should be detailed and plainly readable as Earth, with both Americas visible. The familiar Arctic GC is still the dominant silhouette.
