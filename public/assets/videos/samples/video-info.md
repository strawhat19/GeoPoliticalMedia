## sleep-paralysis.mp4

**Title**

```text
Why You Wake Up but Can't Move | Sleep Paralysis
```

**Description**

```text
You're aware of the room. You try to move. Nothing happens. Why can waking up feel like you're still trapped in a dream?

Sleep paralysis can happen when awareness returns while REM sleep's muscle stillness briefly continues. This short explains the unsettling sense of a presence, why dreamlike sensations can feel real, and what may help while an episode passes.

There is no guaranteed instant escape. If episodes keep happening or leave you very sleepy during the day, talk to a clinician.

#SleepParalysis #SleepScience #Shorts
```

Medical background: [NHS: sleep paralysis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sleep-paralysis/). [Production research](production/research/sleep-paralysis.md).

## india-beneath-tibet.mp4

**Title**

```text
Is India's Plate Tearing Beneath Tibet?
```

**Description**

```text
The Himalayas are only the part of this collision you can see.

India's tectonic plate is pushing beneath southern Tibet. A 2025 seismic study suggests that parts of its deep mantle layer may be tearing and peeling away from the crust. This animation explores the hidden collision beneath the mountains and the evidence scientists use to reconstruct it.

The proposed deep structure is a research interpretation. The visualization compresses geological time; it does not show India's surface splitting apart.

#PlateTectonics #Himalayas #Shorts
```

Study: [Liu, Shi and Klemperer, Communications Earth & Environment, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-025-02601-w). [Production research](production/research/india-tibet.md).

## handwashing-semmelweis.mp4

**Title**

```text
Your Hands Look Clean. What Did You Miss?
```

**Description**

```text
Your hands can look clean while you miss spots during washing. A fluorescent training lotion helps reveal where.

This simulated UV demonstration connects those missed spots to a turning point in medical history: in 1847, Ignaz Semmelweis introduced chlorinated-lime hand disinfection in a Vienna maternity clinic, and deaths from childbed fever fell.

For everyday handwashing, scrub with soap for at least 20 seconds, including between your fingers and around your nails, then rinse and dry. The glow shown here represents leftover training lotion, not actual germs. Less glow doesn't prove sterile hands.

#Handwashing #MedicalHistory #Shorts
```

Background: [Semmelweis University: historical account](https://semmelweis.hu/english/about-semmelweis-university/semmelweis200/), [CDC: handwashing](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/index.html). [Production research and UV-tracer source](production/research/handwashing.md).

## el-nino-2026.mp4

**Title**

```text
El Niño 2026 Could Be the Strongest Since 1950
```

**Description**

```text
Could the Pacific be building an El Niño stronger than any event in the record since 1950?

NOAA's September 10, 2026 forecast put that chance at 75% for October–December 2026, using its Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI). Exceptional heat at the surface and deeper in the Pacific is helping the event strengthen.

This short explains why forecasts favor a wetter southern U.S. and warmer northern U.S. winter, and how shifting rainfall can change flood and drought risks elsewhere. Regional outcomes remain uncertain: El Niño changes the odds, not the exact weather on your street.

Forecast snapshot: September 10, 2026.

#ElNino #Weather #Shorts
```

Forecast source: [NOAA CPC: September 10, 2026 ENSO discussion](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_disc_sep2026/ensodisc.shtml). [Regional outlook sources and production research](production/research/el-nino.md).

**Publishing note:** This upload describes a dated forecast, not an observed record. Keep the date visible. If publishing after a newer outlook is released, check it before presenting this video as a current update.

## Copy approach

The titles lead with a specific question, surprise, or consequence. Each description opens with its own hook, names the actual topic, and keeps the video's scientific qualifications. This follows YouTube's advice to use concise, accurate titles and unique descriptions with clear topic words. [YouTube title guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en), [YouTube description guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en).
