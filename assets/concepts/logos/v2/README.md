# GeoPoliticalMedia — Logo concepts v2

Three additional directions for the independent news blog, vlog, video channel and studio. This round continues the red, navy and white palette while exploring new shapes beyond v1's orbit, viewfinder and GPM page tile. Earlier versions and the approved identity remain intact.

## Directions

| Concept | Idea | Likely use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 · World Dialogue** | A red globe doubles as a speech bubble, linking worldwide reporting with commentary and interviews. White meridians make the globe legible without photographic terrain. | Main identity, interview avatar, discussion section. |
| **02 · Shutter Signal** | Six interlocking aperture blades pair with two broadcast arcs. One navy blade provides contrast and a clear point of motion. | Production studio, video watermark, streaming channel. |
| **03 · Newsline G** | A squared red G contains dark editorial rules and pairs with a serif masthead. Its broad horizontal geometry gives the publication a more editorial voice. | News header, publication stamp, editorial title card. |

## Palette and editing

- Signal red: `#E52539`
- Deep navy: `#0B1A2B`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

All symbols are native SVG paths, circles and strokes. Wordmarks use editable live text: Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif for World Dialogue and Shutter Signal, and Georgia/Times New Roman/serif for Newsline G. Outline the chosen typography in a design editor before production export if exact cross-device rendering is required. The dark samples keep red unchanged and convert navy symbol details to white; the navy panels belong only to the comparison board.

No application code or deployed assets were changed. Continue any later round in `assets/concepts/logos/v3/` and preserve v1 and v2.
